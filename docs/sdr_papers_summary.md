## Active Research Milestones

### 1. Review Paper (Paper 1): Empirical Simulation Benchmark
Following the collaborator review (October 2026), the review manuscript will incorporate 1–2 numerical simulation examples comparing the 6 flagship neural network SDR methods (NNSDR, DRNN, DDR, BENN, StoNet, DVSDR):
* **Model 1**: Continuous regression with mixed linear and non-linear component link functions.
* **Model 2**: Multi-class classification with non-linear decision boundaries.
* **Objective**: Provide an objective empirical comparison table and visualization of subspace recovery accuracy across varying sample sizes $n$ and predictor dimensions $p$.

### 2. Project 2: Pretrained-Model Auditing & Representation Sufficiency (`sdr-audit`)
* 🌐 **Public Open-Source Repository**: [github.com/mnshr/sdr-audit](https://github.com/mnshr/sdr-audit)
* **Core Focus**: Auditing representation sufficiency ($Y \perp\!\!\perp \mathbf{X} \mid \mathbf{Z}$), estimating residual subspaces $\mathcal{S}_{\text{res}}$, surgical model repair, and Central Variance Subspace $\mathcal{S}_{\operatorname{Var}(Y \mid \mathbf{X})}$ uncertainty quantification.
* **Literature Base**: 17 validated papers cataloged in [[project2_arxiv_literature_review]] and archived in `ref/`, expanding into foundational transfer learning and UQ literature per advisor guidance ([[Oct 23 2026 SDR]]).
